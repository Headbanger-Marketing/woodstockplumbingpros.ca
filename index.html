FAQs

Questions Woodstock Homeowners Ask

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Are you licensed for plumbing work in Woodstock?

Yes, licensed and insured, with every permit-worthy job filed with the city and inspected under the Ontario Building Code before we call it done.

Do you answer emergency calls in Woodstock?

Yes. Frozen bursts, sewer backups, flooding, and total water loss are answered around the clock across the city and the county routes beyond it.

My water heater failed today. Can it be replaced today?

In most cases yes, since the common tank sizes are carried on our Woodstock trucks, and the swap includes haul-away and the permit.

Should an older Woodstock home be repiped?

If the supply is original galvanized, eventually yes, and rusty water with dropping pressure is that day arriving. We assess honestly and quote the branch or full job needed.

Do you work on wells and septic around Woodstock?

Constantly. Rural Oxford properties get well treatment, pressure tanks, septic building-drain repairs, and barn lines from a crew that farms the county roads weekly.

Is the water in Woodstock hard enough to soften?

In town and especially on wells, yes. Testing tells us the grain count, and the softener is sized to that figure and your household's use.

What do your services cost?

Written quotes before work begins, free of obligation. The number approved for a repair or a full repipe is the number that appears on the final invoice.

How far outside Woodstock do you travel?

Ingersoll, Tillsonburg, Embro, Thamesford, Norwich, Innerkip, and the farm routes threading Oxford between them.

Do you serve areas outside of Woodstock, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Woodstock and nearby communities including Ingersoll, Tillsonburg, Embro, Thamesford, and the surrounding area.
